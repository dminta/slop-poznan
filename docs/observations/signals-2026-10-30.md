# Signal and traffic observations — 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31

## Setup

builder/init messages and no warnings or errors. This initial console check preceded temporary instrumentation.

## Method

Temporary DevTools instrumentation overrides the existing camera's position and look target, together with
`cameraSystem.currentPos`, instead of calling its normal camera update. The player remains at Bałtyk. This changes the camera and
simulation focus without placing a player obstacle at the observed junction. No production source, signal plan,
traffic algorithm or geodata is changed.

A wrapper calls the original traffic update normally, then samples the existing simulation objects at approximately
0.1 simulation-second intervals. Connector-entry detection compares vehicle state before and after every traffic
update rather than only at the sampling interval. Times are simulation seconds, excluding debugger pauses; the
simulation's clamped timestep means wall-clock duration is not an equivalent measurement.

Each segment records signal nodes, pedestrian waiting/movement transitions and tram movement/waiting transitions
within 180 m of the stated focus. Population was not recorded. The stage wrapper advances after twice the largest
nearby signal cycle plus 30 simulation seconds. The first two captures ended at that threshold; at the final
location, the underlying sampler continued until a manual pause. The car-entry analysis uses cars only, excluding
buses. Emergency classification takes precedence over commitment. Previously committed means
`v.commit === incoming junction` in the pre-update snapshot. Ordinary entries exclude both categories.
A non-green uncommitted entry requires individual review: at connector entry, production can proceed after a
refused `mayEnter` when speed is at least 4 m/s and the target lane is valid.
An entry count alone is not a red-light violation verdict.

Signal records expose the shared cached traffic/WALK/display state. They do not by themselves prove that a visible
head faces the correct approach or displays the expected colour. Pedestrian crosswalk-track membership includes
the landings and is not an oriented body-over-zebra measurement. Tram movement/waiting transitions and section
positions support case selection but do not alone establish geometric clearance of an entire tram.

## Results

### Kaponiera

The segment ran from `31.8307` to `181.8386 s`: `150.0079 s`, with 1,410 samples. All six nearby signal nodes
had 60 s cycles. Each recorded all six vehicle phase pairs and steady green, flashing green and red pedestrian
display states for both groups.

There were 124 detected car connector entries: 116 previously committed entries on green, six emergency entries
on green, one previously committed entry on red and one ordinary uncommitted entry on green. The committed red
entry is not evidence of a new uncommitted red-light entry. The single ordinary uncommitted entry was on green;
the small count limits conclusions about that category.

Pedestrian telemetry recorded 191 state transitions, including 52 waiting records and 137 moving records.
There were 43 waiting-to-release transitions on the same node. For example, pedestrian `13` was waiting at
`31.8307 s` and released at `33.0973 s` with WALK true and speed `0.12987 m/s`. Transition counts are not counts
of distinct pedestrians or completed crossings.

There were 59 tram state transitions across seven pool identities. Rendered snapshots showed two trams on curves,
then an empty view of the same curve in a later shot. Telemetry separately records movement transitions; these
screenshots are not a continuous trace proving rear clearance of the full articulated body.

### Most Teatralny

The segment ran from `181.8554` to `331.8625 s`: `150.0071 s`, with 1,407 samples. Both nearby signal nodes
had 60 s cycles and recorded all vehicle phase pairs and steady green, flashing green and red pedestrian display
states for both groups.

All 26 detected car connector entries were previously committed entries on green. This segment therefore does
not supply an ordinary uncommitted car-entry example.

Pedestrian telemetry recorded 218 state transitions, including 40 waiting records and 178 moving records, and
40 waiting-to-release transitions. The latter include unsignalised crossings; they must not all be described as

There were 101 tram state transitions across nine pool identities. A rendered snapshot showed a whole visible
articulated tram on the curve; a subsequent shot showed the curve empty with cars present. This is a sampled
before/after view, not a continuous full-body rear-clearance trace.

### Głogowska/Hetmańska

## Scope and limits

This record covers partial observations of three junctions, one natural demand crossing and a two-node cached
corridor-phase probe on WebGPU. It does not cover WebGL2, a full rendered Św. Marcin green-wave journey, all
signalised junctions or every possible traffic movement. The player standing by a crossing does not request
a pedestrian phase; NPC waiting logic does.

Audio cannot be heard through the provided observation tooling. No acoustic timing, audible range, spatialisation
or correspondence between crossing ticks and displayed heads is marked as passed. Reading audio schedules or
Web Audio state would not substitute for listening.

After capture, the temporary camera and traffic wrappers were restored and the observer property removed; all
three restoration checks returned true. The breakpoint was disabled and the page reloaded; the final console
again contained only eight informational builder/init messages. No regression tests were rerun for this
documentation-only change; no production behaviour changed.
