# Yard lawns match the parks — 2026-11-08

Follow-up to the report that the courtyards round Chłapowskiego (Wilda) and the alleys under the Targi MTP halls still
showed the old, blotchy yard grass instead of the parks' lawn.

## Cause

`buildings.js` laid one thin instanced slab over every `blk.yards` rect (rear yards, light courts, office cores,
fairground alleys, service yards). The material already sampled `parkLawnColorNode()` for its green, but then masked it
with two synthetic layers painted on the same flat plane:

- `mx_worley_noise_float` at 0.085 and 0.16 world-space scales, read as 12 m and 6 m tree crowns (`crowns`, `leaves`,
  `canopy`). Half the slab faded to a darker "mean canopy", and the rest drew hard overlapping circles — the pattern
  that read as dirty patches rather than grass.
- `mx_noise_float` patches for grey/beige patios and light-court paving, also faded by pixel size.

The parks (`city-grid.js` section 6) use `createParkLawnMaterial(false)` on real lawn rings, with no crowns or paving
painted on top. The two surfaces therefore never matched even though both read the same grass colours.

## Change

`js/buildings.js` now builds `yardMat` with `createParkLawnMaterial(false)` — the exact material factory the park lawn
mesh uses — and keeps the instanced slab geometry unchanged (one `unitBox` instance per `blk.yards`, 0.05 m thick,
top at 0.25 m, still receiving shadows). The park material's world-space colour, mowing stripes, turf normal and
blade specular now apply unchanged to every yard, so a courtyard adjoining a park is continuous with it.

Removed with the old surface:

- the `yardSurface` node graph, including both Worley crown scales and the canopy LOD average;
- the painted patio/paving mask and its `grain`/`clump` noise;
- the now-unused `vec4`, `mx_worley_noise_float`, `parkLawnColorNode`, `parkLawnNormalNode` and
  `setParkLawnSpecular` imports.

No other lawn material was involved. `city-grid.js` already uses `createParkLawnMaterial(false)` for park lawns, and
`outskirts.js`'s land carpet paints its osiedle and park green with `parkLawnColorNode()`; neither had the yard crowns.
The change covers all blocks in one place, including 770 (Targi MTP), 848, 1190, 1254 and 1270 (Wilda).

Where real paving is wanted in a courtyard, it should be drawn as geometry in `city-grid.js`/`street-details.js`, not
as noise on the lawn slab.
