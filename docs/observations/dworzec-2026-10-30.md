## Method

The initial uninstrumented console check contained the eight builder/init messages and no warnings or errors.
NPC pedestrians crossed the shared route while vehicles and trams ran in the full scene.

Temporary DevTools instrumentation sampled the existing traffic instance after its normal update. It recorded
vehicle identity, movement, speed and waiting reason, pedestrian positions and WALK, crossing reservations, and
oriented bus-body overlap with the actual painted crossing. Sampling was approximately every 0.1 simulation
seconds; times below are simulation seconds, excluding debugger pauses. Reservations are built before the
current frame's vehicle/pedestrian movement, so a single counter sample is not a geometric clearance test.

The first logger treated `turn` as ambiguous. An additional read of the production `pedsOut` and `yieldOncoming`
predicates separated a left turn held for pedestrians from one held for opposing vehicles. Instrumentation errors
while setting up DevTools were separate from the initial clean application-console check.
