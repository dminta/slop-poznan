# Kit roof audit, 2026-11-13

No shipping-code changes were needed. All 75 kit roofs (54 x-ridge, 21 z-ridge) have outward winding and matching
stored normals after their actual world transforms. Midday browser views found no missing roof slopes or unexplained
face-darkening on the inspected landmarks on WebGPU or WebGL2.

## Geometry audit

Scratch driver: `.agent-task/audit.mjs`, using `tests/helpers/module.cjs` to load the real kit and all eight landmark
area builders before facade conversion/batching removes mesh identity. Geometry/math come from the exact pinned
Three.js commit's `three.core.js`; node materials and TSL are stubbed, and DOM-only mural painting is skipped.
The repository Three stub's `computeVertexNormals` is a no-op, so it cannot validate the stored-normal requirement.
Only scratch source instrumentation attaches roof dimensions/options and the fit site's landmark name.

`audit.json` lists every roof's size, ridge, hip, mesh rotation (`optsRy`), complete parent rotations/scales,
world centre, footprint edge lengths and world ridge direction. Footprint lengths use transformed local edges,
not the world AABB, so turned blocks are measured in their own frame. Every nonzero triangle is checked against
an interior point at half roof height, with positive world y for slopes; stored normals use the inverse-transpose
normal matrix, compared to the transformed geometric normal at all three vertices (3° tolerance).

436 nonzero triangles pass; maximum stored/geometric angle is 0.00000342°. Fourteen zero-area triangles are the
expected collapsed ridge pieces on square pyramids: four real side triangles remain, without holes or shared smooth
normals. They are counted separately, not treated as inward triangles. No actual roof has a negative determinant.
A second run with the entire landmark root rotated 0.73 rad and scaled (−1.2, 0.8, 1.5) also passes all 75 roofs
(`audit-mirrored.json`). It accounts for Three.js's front-face reversal on negative world determinants; raw cross
products reverse under reflection, whereas outward stored normals use the inverse transpose.

## Architectural ridge review, 2026-11-13

The follow-up resolves direction separately from geometric correctness. The original cited descriptions
([Hotel Bazar](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Bazar_w_Poznaniu),
[Collegium Maius](https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collegium_Maius_w_Poznaniu)) describe the mansard and glazed hall,
but do not specify these four ridge axes. The municipal
[Poznań Film Commission hall description](https://poznanfilmcommission.pl/lokacja/collegium-maius/) independently
confirms glazing, not a gable direction.

Primary visual evidence is the GUGiK national orthophoto mosaic, retrieved 2026-11-13 from
[StandardResolution WMS](https://mapy.geoportal.gov.pl/wss/service/PZGIK/ORTO/WMS/StandardResolution).
GetMap parameters: WMS 1.1.1, layer `Raster`, EPSG:4326, PNG, 1200 × 1000; BBOX (lon/lat)
`16.9291,52.4073,16.9309,52.40825` for Bazar and `16.9179,52.40915,16.9203,52.4104` for Maius.
Unmodified responses and full URLs are in `.agent-task/evidence/{bazar,maius}-ortho.png` and adjacent URL files.
North is up; geographic image aspect is stretched, so these are direction evidence, not dimension measurements.
The default mosaic's acquisition date was not established; retrieval date is not flight date.

| Roof | What the orthophoto shows, relative to the front | Confidence / decision |
|---|---|---|
| Bazar west mansard cap, 33.6 × 21.6 m | The west range's ridge follows Marcinkowskiego north–south, meeting the Paderewskiego range at the northwest corner. That is local z, parallel to the west front, despite the model cap's wider x span. | High for axis, medium for the simplified cap profile. **Keep z**; no rise/hip change justified. |

| Maius east side wing, 24 × 46 m | The right range independently shows a continuous longitudinal ridge, parallel to the west range and perpendicular to Fredry, with a hip at the front corner and separate rear pavilion. | High. **Change x → z**. |

The aerial supports longitudinal local-z alignment, but does not establish a simple hipped ridge rather than a vault/rooflight. | Medium for alignment, low for exact profile. **Keep z**, without claiming the current kit hip matches the real profile. |

Only the shared side-wing call in `buildMaius` changes geometry: add `ridge: 'z'`, retaining `hip: 0.5`, dimensions,
positions and 6.5 m rise. Eaves stay 16.5 m and ridges 23.0 m; `MAIUS.top` stays 36.4 m. Bazar's 20.6–21.3 m cap,
`BAZAR.top`, all footprints and all collider calls are unchanged. Call-site comments explain all four decisions.
No kit or other landmark changes. There is no checked-in DSM grid or reusable DSM sampler in the current tools;
the aerial directly resolves the wing axes, so new height sampling was unnecessary.

A Commons interior photo was considered
([Aneta Pawska, 2019-03-17](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Collegium_Maius_-_hol.jpg)), but direct and
thumbnail downloads returned HTTP 403, and web image retrieval returned a cache miss. Its file description was
accessible; its unseen pixels are not evidence. The hall's exact glazed profile remains a narrowly scoped follow-up.
